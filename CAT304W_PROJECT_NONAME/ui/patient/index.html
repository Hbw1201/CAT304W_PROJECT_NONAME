Privacy Policy

System: Osler (MAQ-SCREEN Lung Cancer Early Screening)

Effective Date: 2025

Last Updated: 2025

Osler (“we”, “our”, or “the System”) is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Osler Patient Portal, including AI-assisted screening questionnaires and report generation. This policy is designed in accordance with the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and common international data protection principles.

1. Scope

This policy applies to patients and users who access the Osler web portal, complete questionnaires, view screening results, and manage their profiles.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication

  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Login/session metadata (e.g., timestamps)

Note: Passwords are not stored in plain text. Authentication is handled securely by Firebase Authentication.

2.2 Profile Information (User-Provided)

  • Name / display name
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Email (for account reference)
  • Family doctor (if provided)

2.3 Questionnaire & Screening Data

  • Answers to screening questions (e.g., symptoms, lifestyle, smoking history, exposure factors)
  • AI-assisted screening outputs and risk indicators generated from your inputs
  • Screening timestamps / history

2.4 Technical Data

  • Basic device/browser information
  • System error logs (for debugging and reliability)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and maintain login sessions
  • To deliver questionnaires and generate screening-support outputs
  • To generate and display personalized screening summaries and reports
  • To improve system usability, stability, and performance
  • To protect system security and prevent misuse

We do not use your data for advertising, commercial profiling, or marketing.

4. AI and Automated Processing

Osler uses AI-assisted logic to guide questionnaire flow and generate screening-support insights. Outputs are informational and supportive only.

Osler does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for clinical advice.

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 Storage

  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication
  • User profile and screening data: Firebase Firestore (user-linked documents)

5.2 Security Measures

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS)
  • Access control enforced via authentication
  • Database access rules (Firestore Security Rules)
  • Principle of least privilege for administrative access

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We may disclose data only when:

  • You give explicit consent
  • Required by applicable law or regulatory authorities
  • Necessary to protect system security and integrity

We do not share personal questionnaire content with third-party AI providers for training purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain screening history, or comply with legal/academic requirements. You may request deletion of your account and associated records.

8. Your Rights (PDPA)

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • Request deletion of your account and data

9. Cookies & Local Storage

We may use cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and store non-sensitive preferences. We do not store passwords or sensitive medical data in cookies/local storage.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use of the System indicates acceptance of the latest version.
