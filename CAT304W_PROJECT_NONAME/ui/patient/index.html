Privacy Policy for MAQ-SCREEN

Effective Date: 2025

MAQ-SCREEN ("we", "our", or "the system") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our questionnaire, risk assessment, and report generation services. This policy is designed in accordance with the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our platform, including the web UI, Node.js backend, and Python-based intelligent questionnaire system.

1.1 Account Information (via Node.js + MySQL)

  • Username or email
  • Password (stored in encrypted format)
  • Registration timestamp
  • Login timestamp

1.2 Questionnaire and Health Assessment Data

Processed by the intelligent agent system (Python):

  • Basic demographic and health-related answers (e.g., age, gender, smoking history, symptoms, lifestyle factors)
  • Responses to screening questions
  • AI-generated analysis and risk assessment outputs

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Access logs (date, time, and basic technical information)
  • Browser and device information
  • System error logs

We do NOT collect extremely sensitive data such as NRIC/IC number, home address, bank information, or biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Provide Core Services

  • Account creation and login authentication
  • Questionnaire delivery and automated screening
  • Risk estimation and personalized report generation
  • Improving accuracy and responsiveness of the system

2.2 To Improve System Performance

  • Usage analytics for service quality enhancement
  • Debugging and fixing errors
  • Enhancing question relevance and flow

2.3 Compliance With Legal Obligations

If required under applicable laws, we may disclose information to regulatory authorities.

3. Data Storage and Protection

3.1 Storage

  • Account credentials are stored in a local MySQL database.
  • Questionnaire responses and generated reports are stored on secure backend servers.

3.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices, including:

  • Password hashing (secure, irreversible encryption)
  • Restricted access control for server environments
  • Secure communication protocols (e.g., HTTPS, encrypted transport)
  • Regular system updates and security patches

3.3 Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal requirements. Users may request data deletion at any time.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
  • Upload your questionnaire content to third-party AI platforms

Data may only be shared under the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • If required by law or regulatory authorities
  • To ensure system security (e.g., detecting malicious access attempts)

5. Your Rights (Under PDPA)

You have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to request deletion of your account and related records

Requests may be submitted via email (see Section 9).

6. Cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Analyse anonymized usage patterns
  • Improve user experience

You may disable cookies in your browser at any time. Cookies do not store sensitive data.

7. Third-Party Services

Our system may interact with:

  • Standard web technologies
  • Node.js backend services
  • MySQL database
  • Python-based analytical modules

However, your questionnaire data is never shared with external AI providers unless explicitly stated.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect system improvements or legal requirements. Updates will take effect immediately upon publication on the platform or accompanying documentation.
